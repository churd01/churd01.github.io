About Me

Hi, I’m Claire Hurd (she/her), and I’m graduating from Allegheny College in May 2025 with a major in Theatre, focusing on Stage Management and Lighting Design, and a minor in Computer Science. I’m passionate about creating organized, inclusive, and collaborative spaces in theatre where everyone feels empowered to do their best work.

At Allegheny’s Playshop Theatre, I’ve had the opportunity to stage manage Machinal and Head Over Heels, assistant stage manage Proof, and production manage Eurydice and This Random World. I’ve also worked as an assistant lighting designer on A Doll’s House, Part 2 and Eurydice. In the summer of 2024, I was the Stage Management Apprentice at Transcendence Theatre Company, where I supported three musical-theatre-inspired concerts.

My work is driven by a deep belief in the power of communication, inclusivity, and professionalism. For my senior comprehensive project, my project, On Boarding the Playshop Theatre, is a production manual designed to document and enhance our theatre department’s process. It offers suggestions to support student experience and build a more positive, sustainable, and welcoming environment for all.

Beyond the stage, I’ve been actively involved in the Allegheny Student Chapter of the United States Institute for Theatre Technology (USITT), serving on the board and attending the USITT national conference each year of my college career. These conferences have been invaluable in growing my network, staying current in the field, and gaining fresh perspectives on technical theatre.

In Fall 2024, I studied abroad at Queen Mary University of London through their London and the Arts Program, where I focused on theatre and culture. Studying in London deepened my appreciation for global performance styles and inspired me to think critically about storytelling in different cultural contexts.

On campus, I’ve also been proud to serve as the Vice President of Finance for my sorority, Kappa Alpha Theta, for three semesters. In this role, I managed and organized our $30,000 annual budget, gaining experience in leadership, financial planning, and organizational communication—skills that directly support my work in production management.

Outside of my academic and theatrical life, I enjoy spending time with friends and family, reading, crocheting, and baking. Whether I’m in the booth, backstage, or at home making cookies, I’m always looking for ways to learn, connect, and create with care and intention.
